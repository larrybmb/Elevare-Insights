How it works

Behavioural science,
not a survey.

We build and run independent owner communities in the markets brands are entering. Owners speak candidly because it isn't the brand's channel — that's why the data is clean.

Owners have always shared this with each other. We read thousands of those conversations and decisions continuously, through recognised behavioural frameworks: who is hesitating, why, and what moves them. Read one at a time it looks incidental. Read as a pattern, it should be informing capital decisions, not just marketing ones.

It matters most in a new market, where partners stand between you and your drivers and you don't yet know what the market expects, fears or means. We translate that context, not just the words.
